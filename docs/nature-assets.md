# Nature assets

## Collectible desk garden

`src/tree-model.ts` generates fifty original miniatures from the catalog in
`src/tree-varieties.ts`: ten silhouettes with five named variations each,
including willows, palms, flowering trees, flowers, succulents, cacti, ferns,
mushrooms and spiral topiary.
Geometry is baked into at most five meshes per specimen. No model download is needed.
The whole pot travels to the spare table; its position follows either seat.
The table fits fifty specimens, with the full collection retained in the database.

The first three plants take 30 minutes each and the fourth takes 1.5 hours.
Later durations vary, and all fifty total exactly 1,000 hours. The collection
stops growing when all fifty varieties are collected. Only visible time aboard
counts. Reduced motion disables sway and completes transfers immediately.

`tree_gardens` stores the current specimen, variety and elapsed growth;
`collected_trees` stores each completed specimen with its owner and collection
time. Growth follows the server's clock: each check-in from a visible tab
(every 15 seconds, and when hiding/leaving the page) adds the time since the
previous check-in, if that was at most 90 seconds earlier. The first check-in
after returning to the tab only restarts the clock. Reset is a transaction: it
counts the time since the last check-in, only a mature current plant can be
collected, and retrying an old specimen ID returns the current state without
duplicating it.
Guest collections transfer on sign-in; the active plant with more accrued growth time is retained
when both profiles already have one. Signing out starts a fresh guest garden.
The obsolete `night-line:tree-age` browser preference is no longer used.

The previous `public/assets/potted-tree.glb` remains as a legacy asset, but is
not loaded by the desk garden. It was converted from `CommonTree_1.fbx` in
[Quaternius: Ultimate Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html)
(CC0), from the creator's
[FBX folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uoIaSvBzm8SrC7g-feRK6ewzHVUGApE0).

## Original landscape foliage

The woodland and coastal planting is generated by `src/nature-models.ts`.
Quaternius' [Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html)
was a reference for variety and branching silhouettes; its landscape models and
textures are not shipped. The initial imported-tree experiment was replaced with
original geometry to suit Night Rail's softer, quieter palette.

The nine forms include two slender trees, broadleaf and muted warm-leaf trees,
flowering and plain shrubs, two small wildflower clumps, and curved grass blades.
Trees have tapered curved trunks and irregular, solid foliage crowns. Vertex
colors shade their undersides, with no alpha-cutout leaf textures or model downloads.

`src/nature-layout.ts` plants deterministic clusters along the route, preserves
clearings around wildlife, cottages and ponds, and samples the actual terrain.
Coastal planting stays inland; snowy and desert regions keep their existing
vegetation. Bounded instanced batches recycle behind the carriage, and a shared
vertex shader adds subtle wind while keeping roots fixed. Reduced motion removes
the sway. Near woodland pines and coarse grass give way to this planting while
the simpler trees remain on distant hills. Near, middle and outer canopy bands
sit above broad, low shrubs and a light bank of grass near the carriage.
Shrubs use two simple smooth masses, and grass clumps use five broad blades.
Small flowers use solid triangular petals. The experimental procedural ground
texture and fern carpet were removed to keep the landscape quiet and inexpensive
to render; the terrain retains its existing vertex colours.
Plant layouts cache the overlapping route sections so recycling only calculates
one new section. A geometry-budget test caps the planted forest below 400,000
triangles at representative route positions.

## Wayside hamlets

`src/settlements.ts` builds original cabins, barns, two-story houses and railway
signal houses. Small groups alternate sides of the line; coastal groups stay
inland. Pitched roofs, timber trim, porches and warm windows give them distinct
silhouettes. Snow roofs follow the region, and stone foundations extend down to
the terrain. Each building merges its details into four meshes, and twelve
buildings recycle across the journey. `src/settlement-layout.ts` shares their
footprints with the planting systems so trees and undergrowth leave room for them.

Fixed carriage furnishings, exterior carriage details and landmark trim are
merged by material through `src/static-meshes.ts`. Animated window panes, wheels'
bogie pivots, smoke and wildlife remain separate. The 3D canvas caps its pixel
ratio at 1.5; HTML interface elements retain native display resolution.
