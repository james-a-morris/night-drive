# Cabin material artwork

Generated with the image generation tool for Night Line. Saved as
`public/assets/train-cabin.png`. The app samples two small painted wood regions
as textures for its Three.js table and wooden trim. The cabin and landscape
remain real 3D geometry; the artwork is not a background image.

The Night Line wordmark uses a locally hosted Latin glyph subset of Cormorant
Garamond Light Italic by Christian Thalmann. It is distributed under the SIL
Open Font License; see [the included license](cormorant-license.txt). The window
and moon emblem is drawn directly in SVG.

## Generation prompt

Asset type: transparent foreground overlay for a cozy lo-fi train study app, wide landscape 16:9 composition, high quality hand-painted 2D game environment.
Primary request: first-person view from a passenger window seat inside a warm night train. Most of the picture must be EMPTY TRANSPARENT WINDOW so an animated landscape can be displayed behind it. The window is enormous and panoramic, occupying the upper 78% of the picture from almost edge to edge. Do NOT paint an outside landscape. Actual transparent alpha through the entire window and window glass, not a checkerboard.
Style: softly illustrated lo-fi animation background, painterly gouache texture, delicate warm linework, sophisticated quiet cozy mood, beautifully observed natural materials. Not low-poly 3D, not photorealistic.
Composition: thin dark walnut train window frame along the top and side edges, gathered soft sage linen curtains only in the outermost 5% on left and right, a few small amber fairy lights along the very top edge. A narrow wooden window ledge and warm wood study table across the bottom 22%. The center of the tabletop must be clear, with no laptop, no monitor, no objects in the middle, because a live interactive laptop will be added there by the app. At bottom-left, a small glazed ceramic tea cup, a little leafy plant, soft light. At bottom-right, a stack of two softly worn books, corner of a knitted blanket. Table objects may rise a little into the bottom of the window but never block more than the lowest quarter. Interior only hugs the edges of the image, like a picture frame around the outside view.
Lighting: amber lamplight gently pooling on the desk, warm terracotta and soft olive textiles, deep walnut, subtle cream highlights, calm dusky shadows. Foreground must feel intimate, warmly lit, personal and illustrated, not beige and flat.
Constraints: NO people, NO train exterior, NO adjacent train cars, NO landscape in window, NO text, NO logo, NO lettering, NO laptop or computer, NO opaque background beyond the window. TRUE ALPHA TRANSPARENCY in the large window area. Make the outside-view opening exceptionally broad and unobstructed.
